## Interpretation (2026-10-22)

- **Weighting matters for "how much" questions, as expected.**
  - ATUS oversamples weekends: 48% of diaries but 29% of days once weighted.
  - Unweighted averages therefore understate the average day's work (153 vs 195 min) and misstate other
    amounts. Any population-facing amount must be weighted.
- **The rhythm findings are robust to weighting.**
  - Shares of days in each rhythm band move by at most 2 percentage points, within about 1.5 SE.
  - Mean wake time, sleep midpoint and bedtime move by at most 0.15 h (~9 min).
  - Group shares move by at most 1.5 points.
  - This fits the weekday/weekend result: the rhythm axes are nearly neutral to day type.
- **The separation conclusions are unchanged by weighting.** Amount-latent totals R² and timing-latent clock
  gain differ by ≤ 0.01 between unweighted and weighted scoring.
- **Limits.** Validation subset only (20% of 2022 respondents), so SEs are wider than full-sample ones. The
  model was trained unweighted, which is appropriate for representation learning; this is a reporting
  sensitivity check, not a weighted model.
