# v4: learning groups with a mixture prior on the timing latent (plan, written before training)

Started 2026-10-21. Development on 2022 train/validation only; no held-out test year remains, so all
results here are development results.

## Motivation

With "train the VAE, then k-means the timing latent", group reproducibility across model sets plateaued at
ARI ≈ 0.3-0.4 for every k = 2-10. Training longer (80 epochs) and ensembling did not move the ceiling
(`docs/v3_k_sweep_ensembles.md`). We try once to make the model organise the timing latent around groups.

## Single change from v1 (80-epoch baseline settings)

- The timing prior p(z_t) = N(0, I) is replaced by a learned Gaussian mixture Σ_c π_c N(μ_c, diag σ_c²)
  (VaDE / GMVAE style; Jiang et al. 2017, Dilokthanakul et al. 2016).
- Timing KL term:
  Σ_c γ_c KL(q(z_t|x) ‖ N(μ_c, σ_c²)) + KL(γ ‖ π), where γ_c = q(c|x) ∝ π_c N(z_t; μ_c, σ_c²).
  Same reduction convention as before (per diary, divided by T).
- Schedule: 20 epochs with the standard prior, then the mixture is initialised by a diagonal GMM fitted
  to train-split timing posterior means (seeded), then 60 more epochs with the mixture prior.
  `best.pt` is selected only after the switch.
- Everything else identical to v1: amount latent, pairs, λ_invariance = 0.3, amount head, decoder,
  optimiser, 80 epochs total.
- A day's group is argmax_c γ_c at its posterior mean, i.e. the model's own assignment (no k-means).

## Runs

- Real 2022 data: K = 4 and K = 6, seeds 42/43/44.
- **Null check:** synthetic diaries generated by a frozen 80-epoch v1 model (`checkpoint5_final/guided_s42`)
  from z ~ N(0, I) (a single Gaussian, so no discrete groups by construction), decoded with self-budgeted
  decoding. Same train/val sizes as 2022. GMVAE K = 6 on these, seeds 42/43/44.

## Evaluation (2022 validation; null models on null validation)

- **Group reproducibility:** cross-seed ARI of the model's own group labels (3 pairs); ARI on confident days
  (top half of max γ in both models); component usage (no empty components).
- **Real vs null:** reproducibility on real data must clearly exceed reproducibility on null data. Otherwise
  stability is not evidence of real groups.
- **Meaning:** among same-total pairs (≤ 60 min), sleep-midpoint difference of split vs kept pairs; share of
  totals vs timing variance explained by groups.
- **Separation and information kept:** amount totals R², amount and timing clock gain beyond totals, decoder
  dependence, transform sensitivity, CE (v3 metrics).
- **References:** v1 80-epoch single models (k-means on the timing latent, ARI ≈ 0.2) and 3-model
  ensembles (≈ 0.25-0.41 depending on k).

**Success** means reproducibility clearly above both the v1 ensembles (~0.4) and the null, with separation
metrics intact and same-total days still split by rhythm. **Otherwise** we fall back to engineered features
for grouping and the VAE for separation and rhythm analysis.
