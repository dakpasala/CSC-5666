fitted on 2022 train rows; 1,627 validation diaries; 179 near-identical-totals pairs (≤ 60 min).

- `ARI_A_vs_B`: agreement between two independent model sets (all days); `ARI_core_days`: on days both sets

- `midpoint_ratio`: among same-total pairs, sleep-midpoint difference of pairs split by the groups vs kept together.

## Interpretation (development result; 2022 validation only)

- **k = 4 is the most reproducible useful setting.**
  - Ensemble A vs B ARI is 0.52, against 0.38 at the k = 6 we had been using.
  - On "core" days, which both ensembles assign confidently (37% of days), agreement is 0.96.
  - The smallest group is 10% (no tiny groups).
  - Among same-total pairs, groups still split days by rhythm: sleep-midpoint ratio 2.04, against 1.48 for
    engineered features at k = 4.
- **k = 2 is also stable** (core 0.99), but it only separates two broad rhythms. k = 10 scores well too (0.48)
  but has 2-3% groups. ARI is non-monotonic in k, and only one A-vs-B comparison exists, so k = 4 is the best
  candidate rather than a proven optimum.
- **The groups are about timing, not amounts.** VAE groups explain ≤ 2% of the variance in activity totals;
  engineered-feature groups explain 8-18%.
- **The instability is concentrated in borderline days.** Confident days are almost perfectly reproducible.
  Only about a third of days are confidently assigned by both ensembles, which is consistent with daily
  rhythms forming a continuum with clear cores.

**The four groups (ensemble A; ensemble B finds the same groups after label matching, 80% of days in the same group):**

| Group | Share A / B | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G2 early/standard | 39% / 42% | wake ~6-7, daytime work, asleep by ~23:00; sleep centred ~02:05 |
| G3 midday-shifted | 30% / 26% | similar wake, long afternoon/evening leisure, later meals |
| G1 late rhythm | 21% / 22% | asleep until ~9-10, active until ~midnight; work centred ~15:10; sleep ~04:20 |
| G0 irregular / daytime sleep | 10% / 10% | sleep extends into the afternoon, wakefulness or work at night (night-shift, naps) |

Most cross-ensemble disagreement is between G2 and G3: adjacent rhythms on a continuum.

been used, this choice of k is a development result. It has not been confirmed on held-out data.

## Correction (2026-10-21): the k = 4 result did not replicate

A follow-up (`docs/v3_k_sweep_ensembles.md`) trained six more v1 models at 80 epochs (identical settings) to
get three disjoint 80-epoch ensembles, i.e. three pairwise comparisons instead of one.

- At 80 epochs, k = 4 is among the **least** reproducible settings: ARI 0.25 ± 0.01, core days 0.60.
  The 40-epoch value of 0.52 rested on a single comparison and appears to have been a favourable draw.
- Across k = 2-10, ensemble reproducibility stays in the 0.25-0.48 range, with no clear optimum. It is
  non-monotonic and noisy, and longer training does not raise it.
- Ensembles still roughly double single-model agreement at every k (~0.4 vs ~0.2). Confidently assigned
  days remain much more reproducible (core ARI 0.6-0.98) than borderline days.

The earlier claim that "k = 4 is the most reproducible useful setting" is withdrawn. The four-group
description above remains a valid *description* of one ensemble's partition, not a reproducible structure.
