## Interpretation (2026-10-22)

- **Day type is not what the rhythm axes measure.** Weekday vs weekend explains 0.3% of axis 1 and 1.1% of axis 2.
  Weekend days do differ, but mostly in *amounts*: 52 vs 247 work minutes (−0.82 SD), which the timing latent
  is built to exclude. They also wake ~50 min later (+0.52 SD) and have fewer transitions (−0.39 SD).
- **Separating day types does not reliably make groups more reproducible.** At k = 4, stratified ARI is higher
  (weekday 0.25 → 0.32, weekend 0.25 → 0.41), but with large spread (SD up to 0.14). At k = 6 it is lower on
  weekdays (0.43 → 0.30) and unchanged on weekends (0.40 → 0.41). Mixing day types is therefore not the main
  cause of group instability, and the continuum conclusion stands.
- Practical note: when describing a day's rhythm, report day type alongside the axes. Weekend days sit
  slightly later on axis 2 (+0.21 SD) and wake later, but within-type variation dominates.
