# Daily rhythm as a continuum: reproducible axes (2022 validation; development result)

Generated by `scripts/continuum_axes.py`. Nine frozen 80-epoch v1 models (seeds 42-50). Per model: PCA of
the timing posterior means fitted on 2022 train rows; each model's 3-axis space rotated onto seed 42's
(orthogonal Procrustes, fitted on train scores only). Consensus axes = principal axes of the 9-model
average, signed so that 'high' means a later sleep midpoint. Bands = terciles of train-set scores.

## Reproducibility of each axis across models

- `matching_only_*`: axes matched one-to-one without rotation, for comparison.
- `single_*`: after Procrustes alignment, across all 36 pairs of single models (Spearman |ρ| of day
  scores; same band; Cohen's κ).
- `consensus_*`: across 3 disjoint 3-model consensus sets (42-44, 45-47, 48-50).
- For reference: hard k-means groups gave ARI ≈ 0.2 (single) and 0.25-0.41 (3-model ensembles).

## Interpretation (2026-10-22)

- **Axis 1 is a reproducible rhythm dimension: earlier-shifted ↔ later-shifted day.**
  - "Low" days concentrate work in the morning and midday and wind down early (work centre ~11:30, bedtime
    ~22:00). "High" days push work and activity later (work centre ~14:00, bedtime ~23:05, wake ~7:55).
  - Across three disjoint 3-model consensus sets, day scores correlate at ρ = 0.90, and 78% of days land in
    the same tercile band (κ = 0.67). Single models already agree at ρ = 0.74.
  - That is far more reproducible than hard groups (ARI 0.25-0.41 for ensembles).
- **Axis 2 is a moderately reproducible "morning start" dimension:** early and busy (wake ~6:25, long morning
  work, more transitions) ↔ late and consolidated (wake ~8:10, longer leisure blocks, fewer transitions).
  Consensus ρ = 0.70, band agreement 62% (κ = 0.43).
- **Axis 3 is weakly reproducible** (ρ = 0.60) without a clear interpretation, so it is not used.
- **The axes are about timing, not amounts:** |ρ| ≤ 0.16 with sleep, work and leisure minutes.
- **Procrustes alignment matters.** Axes 2 and 3 explain similar variance (11.7% vs 10.5%) and rotate between
  models. Without rotation, axis 1 agrees at ρ = 0.67 single-model; with train-fitted rotation, 0.74.

**Recommended use.** Describe days by continuous positions on axis 1 (and axis 2 with a caution) from a
multi-model consensus, with low / middle / high bands as soft labels, instead of hard schedule types.

**Limitations.**
- 2022 validation only (development); no held-out year remains.
- Axes come from PCA of the timing latent, so they are composites. Simple measures such as sleep midpoint are
  perfectly reproducible by definition and are easier to explain. The VAE axes are useful as amount-adjusted,
  multi-feature summaries, not as replacements for simple measures.
- Bands are terciles, not natural boundaries. Unweighted. Days, not people.
