## Interpretation (2026-10-22)

**Part 1: rare patterns can be discovered without labels.**
- **Real non-Gaussian structure exists.** The most heavy-tailed ICA directions of the timing latent reach
  excess kurtosis 4-13, against at most 0.41 for a matched Gaussian null.
- **A "nocturnal day" direction is found without labels in every model.** In all 9 models, a component
  ranked 1st-7th of 16 by non-Gaussianity (the unsupervised criterion; usually top 4) separates night-work
  days at AUC 0.85-0.92. Its 3% tail is enriched up to 25× for night work. The same direction also flags late
  risers (AUC 0.86) and late-night leisure (0.78), so it is better described as nocturnal days than as night
  work only.
- **A daytime-nap direction** (seed 42, rank 2) separates nap days at AUC 0.91, with 7× tail enrichment.
- **Limitation:** the *concept* recurs in every model, but the *exact* component does not. Only 0-1 of 8 other
  models match a seed-42 component at |ρ| ≥ 0.7 (tail Jaccard 0.15-0.55). By the plan's strict
  reproducibility criterion (≥ 7 of 9 models at |ρ| ≥ 0.7), no single component qualifies. The per-model
  check shows that each model contains an equivalent nocturnal direction.

**Part 2: the timing latent encodes clock-based patterns well beyond totals** (supervised probes):
- night work 0.98 vs 0.79 (totals only);
- late riser 0.98 vs 0.87;
- daytime nap 0.95 vs 0.72;
- late-night leisure 0.91 vs 0.82;
- early riser 0.87 vs 0.76.

It does **not** add information for fragmentation patterns (split work day 0.81 vs 0.81; fragmented day 0.74
vs 0.79), the known weak spot. Totals plus timing is best for every pattern.

**Part 3: combined typology.**
- **Description:** composition type × rhythm band × pattern flags gives one readable label per day, e.g.
  "work · earlier-shifted · early riser" or "leisure · later-shifted · daytime nap".
- **Pattern concentrations:**
  - work days: early risers 16.5%, split shifts 11.6%, night work 2.9%;
  - leisure days: naps 15.8%, late-night leisure 10.6%.
  Most days carry no flag, consistent with a continuum.
- **Stability:** composition types are stable (ARI 0.93) and the rhythm band agrees for 78% of days across
  model sets. Pattern flags are deterministic definitions.

**Limitations.**
- 2022 validation only (development); unweighted; days, not people.
- Rare patterns have few validation days (night work 1.4% ≈ 23 days), so AUCs for rare labels are noisy.
- Pattern definitions are hand-chosen thresholds.
- ICA tails are small (40-60 validation days).
